QUESTIONS

Before You Reach Out

Everything you need to know about the workbook, mentorship, and who Great Talk Africa is for.

What format is the workbook?

A digital PDF, written to be read on your phone or laptop. Message me on WhatsApp and it's sent straight to you once payment is confirmed.

Is this different from generic career-coach content?

It's built around six shifts I actually lived, moving from EA to Executive Partner to Chief of Staff, with a running case example, reflection questions, and an interactive gut check for every module.

How do the mentorship calls actually work?

Four calls a month, roughly weekly, thirty to forty five minutes each, plus I'm reachable between calls for quick questions. We start with your actual role and your actual principal, not a fixed curriculum.

Can I get the workbook first and add mentorship later?

Yes. Most people start with the workbook, then move into mentorship once they want someone checking their specific moves instead of reading about someone else's.

I'm a founder or CEO, not an EA. Is this page for me?

The page titled “When You Actually Need a Chief of Staff” is written directly for you. You can also see the six workstreams I bring founders in for at abrahamotieno.com/services, and how it's worked elsewhere at abrahamotieno.com/case-studies.
